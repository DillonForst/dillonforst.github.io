Sevenfold AI — Private AI on your Mac

Your local AI workspace

Sevenfold AI, shown as Sevenfold in the Mac app, is a macOS desktop companion for local AI chat, projects, research, coding and creative work. Installed local models run on the Mac. Local AI use does not require a paid hosted AI account.

A companion for your projects

The desktop workspace combines project and chat navigation with seven pairs of animated companions. Tools support documents, reviewed computer actions and music, podcast and video workflows. The person using the app remains in control of actions and permissions.

Optional Google Drive connection

You can connect your own Google account to find files, inspect folder metadata, read or download files you request, and upload new files you approve. Google account information confirms which account is connected. Cloud operations use the internet; local chat and installed local tools can operate offline.

Permissions and control

The Google connection requests drive.readonly and drive.file. Google describes drive.file as managing files used with the app. Sevenfold AI’s current direct upload tools create new files and block overwriting or deleting existing cloud files. Read the Privacy Policy for data handling, retention and how to disconnect.

Commercial release in preparation

Sevenfold AI is being prepared for commercial distribution as a macOS app. This site provides product and privacy information. Public pricing and downloads have not been announced. Any future app purchase price is separate from running installed local models without a hosted AI subscription.
