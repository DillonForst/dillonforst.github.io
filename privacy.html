Sevenfold Privacy Policy

Scope

This policy describes the Sevenfold desktop application’s optional Google Drive connection and this informational website. Effective date: November 17, 2026.

Data the Google connection accesses

After you authorize it, Sevenfold can access Google Drive file and folder names, identifiers and metadata, and file contents needed for operations you request. It uses your Google account name, email address and stable account identifier to confirm the connected account. Approved new-file uploads send the selected file contents and destination information to Google Drive.

Purpose and permissions

Google data is used for requested file, project and assistant features, including finding or reading documents and working with selected material. The connection requests drive.readonly and drive.file. The provider permissions are broader than Sevenfold’s current direct transfer tools: those tools require review for transfers and block deletion and overwriting of cloud files. If you choose a synced folder as a working folder, approved local file changes can be synchronized by that provider.

Local processing and storage

AI inference uses installed local models by default. Persistent cloud credentials are stored in this Mac’s Keychain and loaded into process memory when needed. Chats, imported materials, project information and recovery copies may be stored in the application’s local data or folders chosen by the user. A chosen synced folder follows its storage provider’s own synchronization settings. This website does not receive your Sevenfold chats, files or credentials.

Sharing and Google Limited Use

Sevenfold does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Its built-in Google connection does not provide a model-training pipeline. Google data is used for the features you request. Sevenfold’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Optional online searches, external services and deliberate file transfers involve their respective providers and policies.

Retention and deletion

You control the local app and its stored work. Deleting a chat or project removes it from active app history; local recovery copies and your backups may retain earlier versions. Downloaded or uploaded files remain in their selected locations until you manage them. Sevenfold’s direct cloud tools do not delete Google Drive files.

Disconnecting Google

Use Disconnect Account Locally in Sevenfold to remove its local saved authorization while retaining the app’s client registration. This does not revoke Google’s consent record. You can separately remove Sevenfold’s access through your Google Account connections settings. Disconnecting does not delete cloud files or previously saved local copies.

Website hosting

This informational site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It includes no analytics scripts, advertising, forms or application sign-in. GitHub may process ordinary website request information under its own privacy statement. Links to Google and GitHub open those services.

Contact and updates

For privacy or data-handling questions, contact mydeardillon@gmail.com. Material changes to this policy will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
